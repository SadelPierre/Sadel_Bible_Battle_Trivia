Place optional real audio files here (mp3/ogg/wav) to replace the built-in
synthesized cues. Register them at startup, e.g.:

  audio.registerFile("correct", "/audio/correct.mp3");

Available cue names: click, playerJoined, gameStart, countdownTick, countdownGo,
timerWarning, correct, incorrect, scoreTick, rankUp, roundComplete, winner.

Use royalty-free, properly licensed, or original audio only.
